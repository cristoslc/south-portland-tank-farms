42 sites within 1 mile of a fence line: all 7 public schools, 25 child care programs, 9 senior housing facilities. Kaler Elementary is 0.04 mi from the Pipe Line parcel; Betsy Ross House sits adjacent to the Gulf/Sunoco parcel. Licensed VOC total ~594 t/yr (parcel shading shows each).

Straight-line distances to OSM-mapped fence lines, not walking distances. Fence lines are community-mapped OSM data. Sources: Maine DEP air licenses, OCFS Child Care Choices (9/24/2026), NCES, SPHA, OSM (ODbL). Datasets: GitHub. Research: full report.
How to read this map. Shaded areas are the tank farm structures (individual storage tanks and their parcels, mapped in OpenStreetMap). The dashed amber boundary is the 1-mile fence-line buffer — the union of all tank/parcel outlines extended one statute mile. Every school, child care, and senior housing marker inside that boundary is one of the 41 sites counted in the research (42 counting one facility exactly at the boundary). Click any marker for measured distances and permit details. Click the buffer polygon for its definition.

Click a star for facility name, address, license number, permitted VOC, and renewal window. Click a school / child care / senior marker for its nearest fence line and distance. Click the dashed boundary for the buffer definition. Note the monitor stations (green diamonds) are placed at their named street locations and are approximate — confirm with Maine DEP before relying on exact positions.
